SousBot

The recipe crawler for Sous, a meal planner that helps people find recipes, cook within a weekly budget, and shop for them.

What it does

SousBot reads recipe pages so that Sous can help people find them. Each recipe in Sous shows your site's name and a prominent View full recipe button that sends readers to your page. Sous is meant to send people to your site, not replace it.

SousBot fetches pages in two ways:

  • When a Sous user saves a recipe link. We fetch that one page.
  • Catalog crawls of a small set of food blogs, using each site's sitemap. Pages are only re-fetched when the sitemap shows they've changed.

What we keep

  • The recipe's title, ingredient list, times, servings, cuisine, and its link
  • The address of your recipe photo. We show it from your site and never copy or re-host it.

What we don't keep:

  • Your instructions, headnotes or stories: readers follow the link to your site for those
  • Reviews, comments, ratings or nutrition information

We never use your content to train AI models. Sous's assistant answers from the details listed above and always links to your page.

How SousBot behaves

  • It identifies itself with this user agent: SousBot/0.1 (+https://<this page>)
  • It obeys robots.txt, including any Crawl-delay, and re-checks it at least daily.
  • It makes at most one request to your site every 5 seconds, and catalog crawls run in small batches overnight.
  • It stops when a site returns errors or asks it to slow down (429 or 503).
  • It never tries to get past logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or bot protection, and never pretends to be a browser or another bot.

Blocking SousBot

To stop SousBot from reading your site, add this to your robots.txt: 

User-agent: SousBot
Disallow: /

You can also block only part of your site with a narrower Disallow path. SousBot picks up changes within a day.

Opt-out and removal requests

If you'd like your recipes removed from Sous, or have any other question, email us:

SOUSBOT_CONTACT_EMAIL

  • We reply within 3 business days.
  • A whole-site removal stops all crawling and hides your recipes from Sous search and discovery within 7 days.
  • Single recipes can also be removed on request.
